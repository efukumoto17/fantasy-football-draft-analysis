## Where this data comes from

ESPN's transaction API stores the players involved for only **25 of these 77 trades**. For the other 52 it kept the acceptance record but dropped the item list, so those trades cannot be read from the transaction log at all.

Those were recovered from the weekly roster snapshots, which are reliable — starter points reconcile to the reported team score on all 672 matchup sides, 2022–2025. A recovered trade is a week-over-week ownership change with no waiver or free-agent add behind it, where two teams both gave and received a player. Only 8 of 180 such moves had any add record, so this is not picking up waiver churn.

Trades marked ⚠︎ are recovered ones. Because a snapshot only places them between two weeks, they carry a week range instead of a timestamp, and the exact date is unknown.
