## Caveats

- Schedules are not balanced — some pairs meet twice a season, others once, so meeting counts vary. A 4–0 over four meetings is thinner evidence than 6–2 over eight.
- Playoff games are included in every record here, unlike the luck-adjusted standings, which use the regular season only.
- Head-to-head says who won, not who was better. Cross-reference all-play % in `luck-adjusted-standings.md`.
