# Luck-Adjusted Standings — 2022–2025

What the record would look like if the schedule were fair. Regular season only (weeks 1–14), 56 games per manager.

## Method

In a 10-team league you play one opponent a week, but you scored against the whole league whether you played them or not. **All-play** records each team against all nine others every week — 5,040 comparisons over four seasons — which strips the schedule out entirely.

- **All-play %** — share of those head-to-heads won. This is the honest measure of how good a team was.
- **Expected wins** — all-play % × games played. What the record *should* have been.
- **Luck** — actual wins minus expected wins. Positive means the schedule was kind.

Luck sums to zero across the league by construction: every lucky win is somebody else's unlucky loss.

## Caveats

- All-play assumes every week counts equally. It does not know you rested starters in week 14 with a bye locked up.
- Expected wins measures scoring, not roster construction. A team that scores well and loses is unlucky, but scoring well is itself partly luck.
- Regular season only. Playoff results are excluded because the bracket is not a fair sample — see the rivalry report for head-to-head including playoffs.
- Luck here is schedule luck. It says nothing about injuries, which show up as lower scoring rather than as luck.
