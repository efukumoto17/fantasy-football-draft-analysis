How to use this

Every round names one position. Go down that round's list and take the first player still available — they're already in my order of preference, so you never have to compare across positions.

"We're higher by" is how many draft slots above the market I have that player. A +64 means the field takes him 64 picks later than I rank him — he'll be sitting there, and he's worth more than his draft slot suggests.
