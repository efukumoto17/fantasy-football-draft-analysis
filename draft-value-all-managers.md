# League-Wide Draft Value (2022–2025)

Who actually drafts well — every manager's picks scored in your league's scoring, versus what each draft slot should return. 4 seasons, ~640 skill picks.

**Method:** actual season fantasy points (league scoring) for every drafted player, 2022–2025. Each pick is compared to the **league average points for that round** (so it's relative to what the other 9 managers got at the same cost), and each player's **positional finish** is compared to where they were taken. Matching is name **+ position** (this corrects same-name collisions — e.g., QB Josh Allen vs. an LB Josh Allen, QB Lamar Jackson vs. a DB Lamar Jackson). The value numbers are zero-sum by construction (they sum to 0 across the league), so positive = you beat the field at that cost, negative = you trailed it.

> **Caveat:** this is *draft-day value realized* — it rewards hitting on picks and is hurt by injuries (Christian McCaffrey 2024, Malik Nabers 2025, etc.), which are luck, not process. It doesn't measure waivers, trades, or actual wins.

## League-wide takeaways

- **The draft is won in the middle and late rounds, not the first two.** Early-round value is small and slot-dependent (elite players go regardless of skill). The separation comes later: the top 3 all crush either the **middle** (Darwin +669) or the **late** rounds (Prashanth +609, Chan +500).
- **Each of the top 3 has a distinct edge:**
  - **Darwin = middle-round king** (+669 in R4–9, more than double anyone else there). His whole advantage is Rounds 4–9.
  - **Prashanth = late-round king** (+609 in R10+). Mines WR breakouts off the waiver-tier (Nico Collins R15, Jordan Addison R11).
  - **Jeffrey Chan = balanced closer** — strong in both the middle (+537) and late (+500).
- **The bust profile is the same across the league:** injured or aging star names taken early-to-mid. CMC '24, Malik Nabers '25, Tyreek Hill '25, J.K. Dobbins '23, Chris Olave '24, Cooper Kupp — these sank multiple managers.
- **The steal profile is also the same:** young, ascending pass-catchers in the late rounds. Brian Thomas Jr. (R11→WR4), Ladd McConkey (R10→WR12), Nico Collins (R15→WR12), Zay Flowers, Chris Olave (R10→WR6), George Pickens (R8→WR5). **Whoever nails these wins the draft.**

## Manager capsules (best → worst)

### 1. Jeffrey Chan — the best drafter in the league (+1238)
Elite in both the middle (+537) and late (+500). A WR-first drafter who consistently finds mid/late value.
- **Steals:** Chris Olave (R10→WR6), Jakobi Meyers (R15→WR18), Raheem Mostert (R13→RB5).
- **Busts:** James Conner (R5→RB81), Evan Engram (R7→TE33).
- **Tell:** rarely reaches; turns Rounds 8–15 into starters.

### 2. Prashanth Balaraman — the late-round master (+1154)
Best late-round drafter in the league (+609). Steady elite-WR anchor up top, then hits on breakouts.
- **Steals:** Nico Collins (R15→WR12), Jordan Addison (R11→WR22), Deebo (R12→WR25).
- **Busts:** mid-round WRs — Cooper Kupp (R1→WR40), Xavier Worthy (R6→WR59), Dameon Pierce (R5→RB55).
- **Tell:** patient; wins the last third of the draft.

### 3. Darwin Hu — owns the middle rounds (+1064)
The middle-round king (+669 in R4–9 — the single most dominant phase number in the league). Flat late.
- **Steals:** Jaxon Smith-Njigba (R9→WR9), DJ Moore (R7→WR6), Travis Etienne (R12→RB10).
- **Busts:** early RBs — Jonathan Taylor (R1→RB33), Rhamondre Stevenson (R4→RB35).
- **Tell:** his QB-early pivot frees him to feast on falling RB/WR in Rounds 4–9.

### 4. Ikaika Stone — dead average (−19)
No strong phase, no glaring weakness — right at the baseline.
- **Steals:** Chase Brown (R12→RB11), Stefon Diggs (R11→WR17), Javonte Williams (R13→RB12).
- **Busts:** Calvin Ridley (R7→WR117), Allen Robinson (R7→WR83).

### 5. Evan Fukumoto — the barbell (−89)
Best-in-league early (+363), **worst-in-league in the middle (−787)**, strong late (+343). Elite top-of-draft and late fliers, but Rounds 4–9 erase it. *(Full breakdown in `draft-pick-performance.md`.)*
- **Steals:** Jordan Love (R12→QB5), Jameson Williams (R16→WR21), Pittman (R13→WR19).
- **Busts:** Trey Lance (R6→QB61), Cam Akers (R7→RB70), Christian Watson (R5→WR68).
- **Tell:** aging vets and mid-round QBs in Rounds 4–8.

### 6. Chase Mizoguchi — hurt by early injuries (−490)
Only manager besides Darwin with a positive middle (+210), but a bad early (−273) and the second-worst late (−404), largely injury-driven.
- **Steals:** Zay Flowers (R9→WR7), Jameson Williams (R8→WR12), Josh Jacobs (R7→RB3).
- **Busts:** Tyreek Hill (R5→WR106), Brandon Aiyuk (R4→WR105), Isiah Pacheco (R2→RB65).

### 7. Christopher Pascual — collapses late (−529)
Good early (+222) and middle (+263), but the **worst late-round drafter in the league (−790)** and a bad R3.
- **Steals:** George Pickens (R8→WR5), Amon-Ra St. Brown (R7→WR7).
- **Busts:** Mike Williams (R6→WR112), Keenan Allen (R3→WR39), Jayden Daniels (R3→QB34).
- **Tell:** his depth picks (R10+) rarely hit — the mirror image of Prashanth.

### 8. Harvey Wang — soft early and middle (−651)
Negative in the first two rounds (−296) and the middle (−274); the hard TE-punt leaves little margin.
- **Steals:** Michael Pittman (R10→WR13), Courtland Sutton (R10→WR15), Meyers (R12→WR24).
- **Busts:** aging WRs — Mike Evans (R5→WR75), Terry McLaurin (R4→WR57), Deebo (R2→WR40).

### 9. Rollin Odama-Wong — the QB anchor isn't paying off (−716)
Despite always taking a QB in Round 1, he's second-worst overall, dragged by the **middle rounds (−598)** where his RB/WR picks bust.
- **Steals:** Baker Mayfield (R13→QB4), Chuba Hubbard (R12→RB15), Jaylen Warren (R14→RB22).
- **Busts:** J.K. Dobbins (R8→RB114), Chris Olave (R4→WR94), Rashee Rice (R6→WR99).
- **Tell:** paying up for QB early leaves his middle-round RB/WR thin, and they've missed.

### 10. Justin Ho — sunk at the top (−962)
Last overall, almost entirely due to the **worst early rounds in the league (−823)** — but mostly injury luck (CMC '24, Nabers '25 in Round 1). Actually excellent at late WR darts.
- **Steals:** Brian Thomas Jr. (R11→WR4), Ladd McConkey (R10→WR12), Gus Edwards (R17→RB25).
- **Busts:** Malik Nabers (R1→WR102), Nick Chubb (R2→RB89), Aaron Rodgers (R6→QB85).
- **Tell:** great late-round eye; brutal Round-1 injury luck two years running.
