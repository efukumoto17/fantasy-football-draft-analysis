# Where Evan Leaks Draft Value (2022–2025)

How your drafted players actually scored — in your league's scoring — versus what their draft slot should return. 4 seasons, your 68 picks, cross-checked against every other pick in the league.

**Method:** pulled each player's **actual season fantasy points** (league scoring, `kona_player_info`) for 2022–2025, computed each player's **positional finish** that year (e.g., RB16 = 16th-best RB), and compared it to where you drafted them. "League avg for the round" = the average points of *all 10 managers'* picks in that round, so every comparison is apples-to-apples. 673/680 picks matched (the 7 misses are zero-point IDP fliers).

## The three recurring bad-pick patterns

### 1. Name-brand aging / injury-prone vets in Rounds 4–8

The market prices these players on name value; you're paying a Round 4–8 cost for players in decline.

### 2. Reaching on a QB in the middle rounds — while your late QBs print
Your **early QB moves (Rounds 1–2) are gold** (Lamar QB1, Hurts QB8). But your **middle-round QBs are disasters**, and your **late-round QBs are your best value in the whole draft:**

| 2022 R6 Trey Lance | QB12→**QB61** | 12 pts | 2023 R12 Jordan Love | QB25→**QB5** | 319 |

In a 2-QB league your board makes QBs *look* like value in the middle, but that's exactly where they've burned you. Take your QB1 early (works) and your QB2/3 **late** (works) — **stop taking QBs in Rounds 4–6.**

### 3. Your WR room is barbelled — studs early, fliers late, a hole in the middle
Your WR hits are all early (Hill, Adams) or late (Jameson Williams WR21 in R16, Pittman WR19 in R13, Zay Flowers, Jayden Reed). Early-round value by position confirms it: your R1–6 **WRs finish ~11 spots below their draft slot** on average, your **QBs ~14 below.**

## What to change

1. **Fix the middle rounds — that's your whole gap.** You're elite at the top and great late; you're last-in-league from Rounds 4–9. That's where the season is won or lost for you.
2. **Stop drafting declining name-brand vets in Rounds 4–8.** Waller, Kupp, Cooper, Mostert, Akers, Aaron Jones — that's a *pattern*, not bad luck. When your board says "recognizable veteran" in the middle, that's your tell to pivot.
3. **Attack the middle with young, ascending players** — the exact profile that's been your best late-round value (Reed, Flowers, Jameson Williams, Pittman, BRob). Move that instinct up a few rounds.
4. **Barbell your QBs on purpose.** Elite QB early (Rounds 1–2) has worked; late QB (Rounds 12+) has been your single best value pocket. The Round 4–6 QB is the one that keeps sinking you — cut it out.
