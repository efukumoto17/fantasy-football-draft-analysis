# Draft Value Deep-Dive — Every Manager (2022–2025)

A full per-manager breakdown of where each drafter makes and loses value, in the same style as the Evan report. Actual season fantasy points (your league's scoring), each pick compared to the **league average for that round** and to the player's **positional finish** vs. where they were taken. Name+position matched to avoid same-name collisions. Value figures are zero-sum across the league (they sum to 0), so **positive = you beat the field at that draft cost.**

**Reading the positional line:** "WR +11" means your early (Rounds 1–6) WRs finished, on average, 11 spots *below* where you drafted them (higher = worse). Near 0 = drafted them right.

> **Caveat:** this rewards hitting picks and punishes injuries (CMC '24, Nabers '25, Aaron Rodgers '23), which are luck, not process. It's a draft-day lens, not a measure of in-season management or wins.

## 1. Jeffrey Chan — the complete drafter (+1238)
**Shape:** strong in every phase except a soft R3; elite in both the middle (+537) and late (+500).
**Positional (R1–6):** QB +4, RB +9, WR +4, TE +6 — **no real weakness anywhere.**
- **Best rounds:** R2 (+65: Davante, Lamar, Nacua), R9 (+59: Geno Smith QB14, Love QB15), R14 (+62: Stevenson RB8, elite D-ST).
- **Steals:** Chris Olave (R10→WR6), Raheem Mostert (R13→RB5), Jakobi Meyers (R15→WR18), Rhamondre Stevenson (R14→RB8).
- **Busts:** James Conner (R5→RB81), Evan Engram (R7→TE33).
- **Tell & fix:** he's the model — turns Rounds 8–15 into starters with no positional blind spot. The only leak is Round 3 (−55: Conner, Mark Andrews finished light). Marginal.

## 2. Prashanth Balaraman — the late-round master (+1154)
**Shape:** the best late-round drafter in the league (+609); steady early and middle.
**Positional (R1–6):** QB +11, RB +7, WR +10, TE +7 — slightly leaky up top, more than made up for later.
- **Best rounds:** R7 (+48: Fields, Stroud, A. Jones — QB/RB value), R15 (+44: **Nico Collins WR12**), R3 (+103: Bijan, McBride, Adams).
- **Steals:** Nico Collins (R15→WR12), Bo Nix (R13→QB7), C.J. Stroud (R12→QB9), Jordan Addison (R11→WR22).
- **Busts:** Dameon Pierce (R5→RB55), Xavier Worthy (R6→WR59), Cooper Kupp (R1→WR40).
- **Tell & fix:** patient WR-anchor who wins the last third with young WRs and late QBs. Weak spot is **Round 5** (−28: reached on Breece Hall twice, Pierce). Tightening R5 is the only obvious gain.

## 3. Darwin Hu — the middle-round king (+1064)
**Shape:** +669 in Rounds 4–9 — the single most dominant phase number in the league — but flat late (+64).
**Positional (R1–6):** **QB +0.5, WR −0.8** (both elite), RB +7. His early QB and WR picks are the best-timed in the league.
- **Best rounds:** R6 (+71: Metcalf, DJ Moore, **Drake Maye QB2**), R4 (+52: Achane, JSN, McLaurin), R3 (+43).
- **Steals:** JSN (R9→WR9), DJ Moore (R7→WR6), Travis Etienne (R12→RB10), Aiyuk (R9→WR14).
- **Busts:** Jonathan Taylor (R1→RB33 '22), Rhamondre (R4→RB35), Dalton Kincaid (R7→TE30).
- **Tell & fix:** taking his QB early (his 2023+ pivot) frees him to feast on falling RB/WR in Rounds 3–9. His only soft spot is the **late rounds (+64, basically flat)** and a Round-7 dip — his R10–18 dart throws lag the top tier. Sharpening late-round upside is his path to #1.

## Cross-league patterns
- **Draft skill is concentrated.** Three managers (Chan, Prashanth, Darwin) are +1,000 or better; everyone else is at/below baseline. The gap is real and repeatable.
- **The top 3 each own a phase:** Darwin the middle, Prashanth the late, Chan both. **Nobody wins on Rounds 1–2 alone.**
- **Two archetypes of failure:** *early boom-bust* (Chase, Justin Ho, Harvey — premium picks that bust/get hurt) and *depth desert* (Pascual, Rollin, Evan's middle — the picks after Round 3 don't hit).
- **The universal lesson:** the managers who win draft **young, ascending pass-catchers late** (Chan's Olave, Prashanth's Nico Collins, Darwin's JSN, Justin Ho's Brian Thomas) and **avoid reaching on QBs and aging names in the middle** (everyone's bust list). Value lives in Rounds 7–15, not Rounds 1–2.
