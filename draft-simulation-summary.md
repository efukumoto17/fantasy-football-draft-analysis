# 2026 Draft Simulation — Summary (Evan, slot 2)

What you'd likely end up with drafting off the **BDGE PPR board** while the other 9 managers draft to **current ESPN ADP** (adjusted for your league's real behavior). 300 simulated snake drafts, run on the **updated board (Sep 3)**.

## Key setup facts
- **You draft at slot 2** (order is locked), snaking right behind Jeffrey Chan.
- **It's a 2-QB league** (2QB/2RB/2WR/1TE/1FLEX/1K/1D-ST, 16 rounds) — which is *why* QBs go early here.
- Opponents draft to ESPN ADP with **QB demand anchored to your league's actual history**, and K/D-ST left to the last two rounds. You draft off your BDGE board.

## The dynamic that drives your draft
Your board still ranks QBs well ahead of ESPN's 1-QB ADP (Burrow #16 vs ADP 57; Lawrence #30 vs ADP 94). But on the updated board the **second QB tier slid ~5 spots** (Hurts, Lawrence, Maye) while several RBs rose — so a QB is no longer *automatically* your best available.

### Scenario A — follow the board literally
Gibbs → K. Walker → **Burrow (R3)** → **Lawrence (R4)** → WR depth. Elite QB tandem, but you reach ~2 rounds early and your WR1 is Tee Higgins in R5. Composition ≈ **3 QB / 4.2 RB / 5.8 WR**.

### Scenario B — board + wait on QB (recommended)
Gibbs → K. Walker → Hampton → **DeVonta (R4)** → **Lawrence (R5)** → **Herbert (R6)**. Same calibre of QBs two rounds later, plus a much stronger RB core. Composition ≈ **3 QB / 5.0 RB / 5.0 WR**.

## True either way
- **Round 1 you get Gibbs 90%** of the time (Bijan the other 10%) — Chan usually opens WR.
- **Round 2 is Kenneth Walker III** in ~100% of drafts.
- **MarShawn Lloyd in Round 10, every single draft** — his #131 → #75 jump against a 167 ADP is the biggest new edge on this board.
- Late QB3 as insurance; K and D/ST in the final two rounds.

## Recommendation
**Wait on QB.** You get comparable quarterbacks either way; waiting buys you Hampton and DeVonta Smith near market price instead of a Round 3 reach.

This used to be advice to fight your board. It mostly isn't anymore — the new board dropped the second QB tier behind the RB/WR names you'd want regardless, so discipline and best-available now agree more often.

**Watch the opposite risk:** this path lands you at 5.0 RB / 5.0 WR, against your usual WR lean. Round 7 (RB 76% / WR 24%) is the cheapest place to rebalance.
