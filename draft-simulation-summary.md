# 2026 Draft Simulation — Summary (Evan, slot 2)

What you'd likely end up with drafting off the **BDGE PPR board** while the other 9 managers draft to **current ESPN ADP** (adjusted for your league's real behavior). 300 simulated snake drafts.

## Key setup facts the sim uncovered
- **You draft at slot 2** (order is already locked), snaking right behind Jeffrey Chan.
- **It's a 2-QB league** (2QB/2RB/2WR/1TE/1FLEX/1K/1D-ST, 16 rounds) — which is *why* QBs go early in your league.
- Opponents draft to **ESPN ADP**, but their **QB demand is anchored to your league's real history** (2 QBs in Round 1, a run in Rounds 6–7), and K/D-ST stay in the last two rounds — like your league always does. You draft off your **BDGE board**.

## The one dynamic that drives your draft
Your BDGE board ranks QBs **~30–70 spots ahead of ESPN's ADP** (Burrow #16 vs ADP 57; Trevor Lawrence #25 vs ADP 94). So a QB is almost always your "best available" — and how hard you follow that is the entire decision.

### Scenario A — follow the board literally
RB1 (Gibbs) → RB (K. Walker) → **Burrow (R3)** → **Lawrence (R4)** → WR depth. You get an elite 2-QB tandem but **reach ~2–3 rounds early on both QBs and end up thin at WR** (WR1 is Tee Higgins in R5). Composition ≈ **3 QB / 3.7 RB / 6.3 WR**.

### Scenario B — board + wait on QB (recommended)
Gibbs → K. Walker → Chase Brown → **DeVonta (R4)** → **Lawrence (R5)** → **Herbert (R6)**. You get the *same* QBs two rounds later plus a much stronger RB/WR core. Composition ≈ **3 QB / 4.4 RB / 5.6 WR**.

## Things true either way
- **Round 1 you almost always get Gibbs** — Chan (a career WR-first drafter at slot 1) often opens with Ja'Marr Chase, letting Gibbs fall to you. Otherwise you get Bijan.
- **You won't be reaching for the top QBs** — Rollin (QB in Round 1 all 4 years) and Darwin sit at slots 8/10 and grab Allen/Lamar/Daniels. Your realistic QB tier is Burrow/Lawrence/Herbert, all gettable Rounds 3–6.
- Following this board makes you come out **RB-heavy, not your usual WR-heavy**, because your top-ranked WRs all carry top-15 ADP and are gone before your Round 2–3 wheel picks.

## Bottom line
Your board's QB love is a useful **value signal** (it flags underpriced QBs), but cash it by **letting those QBs fall to Rounds 5–7** rather than reaching — that keeps the 2-QB edge *and* gives you a real skill-position core.

## Caveats
- Opponent picks are modeled from ADP + your league's tendencies (not literal predictions); QB scarcity is calibrated to your 2024–25 drafts.
- ADP is ESPN's public **1-QB** ADP (no 2-QB feed exists) — that's *why* your QBs "fall," and the sim accounts for it.
- Deep rounds (10–16) are interchangeable fliers; treat the **Rounds 1–7 core** as the real output.
