# 2026 Draft Simulation — Summary (Evan, slot 2)

What you'd likely end up with drafting off the **BDGE PPR board** while the other 9 managers draft the way they actually draft. 300 simulated snake drafts, on the **updated board (Sep 3)** with **opponent models fitted to your league's 2022–25 drafts**.

## Setup
- **You draft at slot 2** (order locked), right behind Jeffrey Chan — who also picks twice in a row at 20–21, between your Round 2 and Round 3 picks.
- **2-QB league** (2QB/2RB/2WR/1TE/1FLEX/1K/1D-ST, 16 rounds) — which is why QBs go early here.
- Opponents are modeled per manager from their real draft history: each one's habitual round for their 1st and 2nd QB, and their early-round positional lean. Validated to **0.29 rounds** of error on QB timing.

### Scenario A — follow the board literally
Gibbs → **Lamar (R2)** → K. Walker → **Burrow (R4)** → WR depth. Elite QB tandem, but your WR1 is Tee Higgins in Round 5. Composition ≈ **3 QB / 3.9 RB / 6.1 WR**.

### Scenario B — board + wait on QB (recommended)
Gibbs → K. Walker → Hampton → WR1 (R4) → **Lawrence (R5)** → **Herbert (R6)**. Comparable QBs two rounds later, plus a real RB core. Composition ≈ **3 QB / 4.2 RB / 5.8 WR**.

## True either way
- **Round 1: Gibbs ~88%** (Bijan ~12%) — Chan usually opens WR.
- **Round 2 (#19): Kenneth Walker ~80–85%, but Lamar Jackson slips to you 13–18% of the time.**
- **MarShawn Lloyd in Round 10, every draft** — #75 on your board against a 167 ADP is your biggest structural edge.
- Late QB3 as insurance; K and D/ST in the final two rounds.

## Recommendation
**Wait on QB — with one exception: take Lamar at 19 if he's there.**

You end up with comparable quarterbacks either way; waiting buys Hampton or Chase Brown and a real WR1 near market price. But Lamar at BDGE 9 / ADP 38 is a different class of value than Burrow or Lawrence, and he reaches you in roughly one draft in six. Burrow at pick 22 is not worth the same break in discipline.

**The RB-heavy warning from the previous version is gone** — that was mostly an artifact of under-modeled opponents. You now land at 4.2 RB / 5.8 WR without steering.

**Caveat:** the sim takes ~1 fewer QB than reality through the first 40 picks, so treat Lamar reaching pick 19 as an optimistic estimate and the case for waiting as strong rather than airtight.
