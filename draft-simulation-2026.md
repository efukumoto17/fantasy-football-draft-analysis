# 2026 Draft Simulation — Evan (slot 2)

What you'd likely end up with if you draft off the **BDGE PPR board** while the other 9 managers draft to **current ESPN ADP** (adjusted for this league's real behavior). 300 simulated snake drafts.

## Setup the sim used
- **Your slot:** 2 (snake) — order is already set: Chan, **You**, Ikaika, Justin Ho, Chase M., Pascual, Prashanth, Rollin, Harvey, Darwin.
- **League:** 10-team, **2-QB** start (2QB/2RB/2WR/1TE/1FLEX/1K/1D-ST + 6 bench, 16 rounds).
- **You:** pick the best available player on your BDGE board (roster-aware — don't hoard a position past need).
- **Opponents:** draft to ESPN ADP, **but** QB demand is anchored to your league's actual history (2 QBs go in Round 1, a QB run in Rounds 6–7), and everyone leaves K/D-ST for the last two rounds — exactly as your league has always done.

## What to expect regardless of approach

- **Round 1:** You almost always land an elite RB — **Gibbs** most often, because Chan (slot 1, a career WR-first drafter) frequently opens with Ja'Marr Chase, letting Gibbs slide to you. If Chan takes Gibbs, you get **Bijan**.
- **You will not be the one reaching for the top QBs.** Rollin (QB in Round 1 all four years) and Darwin sit behind you at slots 8 and 10 and hoover up Allen/Lamar/Daniels early. Your realistic QB tier is **Burrow / Lawrence / Herbert / Daniels-if-he-falls** — all gettable in Rounds 3–6.
- **TE:** your board pushes it to ~Round 14 (Juwan Johnson / Kincaid tier). Consistent with your career-long TE-punt — but in this sim it's forced, since your board never surfaces a TE worth an earlier pick after Bowers (BDGE 23) goes.
- **K / D-ST:** last two rounds, every time (as the whole league does).

## Recommendation for your slot

The **wait-on-QB** version is the stronger use of this board. Your board's QB love is an *edge signal* — it tells you which QBs are underpriced (Burrow, Lawrence, Herbert) — but you should cash that edge by **letting them fall to Rounds 5–7**, not by reaching in Rounds 3–4. Spend Rounds 1–4 on the RB/WR your board ranks highest, then take two of your favorite value QBs back-to-back once the elite tier clears. That gets you the 2-QB advantage *and* a real skill-position core.

### Caveats
- Opponent behavior is modeled (ESPN ADP + your league's historical QB-timing and K/D-ST discipline), not a prediction of specific rival picks. QB scarcity is calibrated to your 2024–25 drafts.
- ADP is ESPN's public **1-QB** ADP (there's no 2-QB ADP feed) — that's *why* your QBs appear to "fall," and the sim accounts for it.
- Deep picks (Rounds 10–16) are interchangeable fliers; treat the **Rounds 1–7 core** as the real output. Underlying data: `.espn-automation/sim_results_strict.json` and `sim_results_waitQB.json`.
